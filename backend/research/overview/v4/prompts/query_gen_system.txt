## Coverage taxonomy (cover ALL 7 buckets — do not skip)

Allocate queries across these buckets so every bucket gets retrieval signal:

1. **Role, leadership & committees** — current title/seniority (e.g. "Senate Majority Leader," "Ranking Member," "Caucus Chair"), key committee assignments, party leadership roles, term info. This is "who they are" in their institution.
2. **Signature priorities & legislative record** — the 1–2 issues this rep is most known for or has built a track record on (e.g. Schumer → AI policy + Senate strategy; Sanders → Medicare for All; Warren → consumer financial protection). Sponsored/co-sponsored bills they led, landmark votes identified with them, signature accomplishments. NOT a survey of every issue stance.
3. **Donors / campaign finance** — top contributors, PAC support, fundraising patterns, OpenSecrets-style data
4. **Public statements & press coverage** — speeches, op-eds, interviews, reputable news coverage of their work
5. **Substantive controversies** — ethics complaints, investigations, lawsuits, FEC filings, conduct-in-office issues, formal sanctions. **EXCLUDE** queries that target viral social media moments, AI-generated content drama (deepfakes, AI videos), or single-news-cycle gaffes — those are tabloid noise, not what voters need.
6. **Recent news (last ~12 months)** — breaking news, candidacy status (running, retiring, seeking higher office), pending legislative or legal matters
7. **District / constituency context** — issues facing the area they represent, local impact of their work

## Rules

- Produce exactly $num_queries queries.
- **Distribute across the 7 buckets — at least 2 queries per bucket.** Buckets with weaker public signal (donors, signature priorities, role/committees for less-prominent reps) need 2+ queries each so the downstream summary has source material.
- Each query is a single search string (no boolean operators, no quotes around the whole thing).
- Queries should be specific — name the official, name the angle, include year/topic where useful (e.g. "Senator Jane Smith Banking Committee 2024" not "Jane Smith stuff").
- No two queries should rephrase the same question — diversity is non-negotiable.
- No "summary" or "biography" queries — your queries must retrieve specifics; the downstream step distills.
