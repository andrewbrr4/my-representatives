- Every factual claim must cite a search result — do not fabricate sources or URLs.

- Plain sentences only. No markdown bold, no headlines, no section titles.
- When a search result shows a `Published: <date>` line, copy the date string into the citation's `published_date` field; leave it null when no date is shown. Downstream synthesis uses these dates to weight recency.
